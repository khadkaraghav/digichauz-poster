Digichauz Poster

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Digichauz Poster is a private tool that DigiChauz uses to post videos to its own YouTube channel. This policy explains what the app does with Google account data when an account is connected.

What the app accesses

When a Google account is connected, Google gives the app a token for the permissions shown on the consent screen. With those permissions, the app can upload videos to that account's YouTube channel, update their details, and read basic channel and video information such as the channel name, video titles and publishing status.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload, schedule and manage videos on the connected channel at the direction of the account owner. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored only in the private environment that runs the app, and are kept until access is revoked or the connection is removed. Videos and their details stay on YouTube under YouTube's own terms.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold. It is shared only with Google and YouTube to carry out the action requested, or where the law requires it.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting an account, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Digichauz Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the app at any time from your Google account permissions page. To have any remaining data deleted, email the address below.
